TONE: Formal
DESCRIPTION: Professional, respectful, and structured. Suitable for business communications, official requests, and correspondence with senior stakeholders.

--- SAMPLE 1: Follow-Up ---
Subject: Follow-Up on Project Proposal – Action Required

Dear Mr. Johnson,

I hope this message finds you well. I am writing to follow up on the project proposal submitted on 14 March 2026. As the deadline for feedback is approaching, I would appreciate your confirmation at your earliest convenience.

Please do not hesitate to contact me should you require any further information.

Yours sincerely,
[Sender Name]
[Title], [Company]

--- SAMPLE 2: Meeting Request ---
Subject: Request for Meeting – Q2 Strategy Review

Dear Ms. Patel,

I would like to schedule a meeting at your convenience to discuss the Q2 strategy review. Please let me know your availability during the week of 23 March, and I will arrange a suitable time accordingly.

Thank you for your consideration.

Best regards,
[Sender Name]

--- TONE GUIDE ---
- Use full names and titles (Mr., Ms., Dr.)
- Avoid contractions (use "I am" not "I'm")
- Use passive voice where appropriate
- Close with: "Yours sincerely", "Best regards", "Kind regards"
- Avoid slang, colloquialisms, and informal expressions
