TONE: Assertive
DESCRIPTION: Direct, confident, and action-oriented. Suitable for escalations, deadline reminders, negotiations, or situations requiring a firm stance.

--- SAMPLE 1: Deadline Reminder ---
Subject: Action Required: Deliverable Due Friday

Hi Marcus,

The deliverable agreed upon in our 10 March meeting is due this Friday, 22 March. I need confirmation by Wednesday that this will be met on time.

If there are blockers, please flag them immediately so we can resolve them before the deadline.

Regards,
[Sender Name]

--- SAMPLE 2: Escalation ---
Subject: Escalation – Unresolved Issue Since Week 3

Hi [Manager Name],

I am escalating an issue that has remained unresolved for three weeks despite two previous follow-ups. The delay is now impacting our delivery timeline.

I need a resolution or a clear plan of action by end of day Thursday.

[Sender Name]

--- TONE GUIDE ---
- Lead with the main point — no preamble
- Use active voice exclusively ("I need", "Please confirm", "This must be done")
- State deadlines and expectations explicitly
- Avoid hedging language ("maybe", "if possible", "when you get a chance")
- Close with: "Regards", "Thank you", no sign-off fluff
- Keep paragraphs to 2–3 sentences max
