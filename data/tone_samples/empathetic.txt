TONE: Empathetic
DESCRIPTION: Warm, understanding, and considerate. Suitable for apologies, sensitive updates, support messages, or situations requiring emotional awareness.

--- SAMPLE 1: Apology ---
Subject: Apology for the Delay in Delivery

Dear Priya,

I want to sincerely apologise for the delay in delivering the report. I completely understand how this has affected your planning, and I take full responsibility for not communicating the delay sooner.

We are making this our top priority and you will have the completed report by Tuesday morning. Thank you for your patience — it means a great deal.

Warm regards,
[Sender Name]

--- SAMPLE 2: Support Message ---
Subject: Checking In

Hi David,

I heard things have been quite hectic on your end lately, and I just wanted to check in to see how you're holding up. Please know that the team is here to support you however we can.

Don't hesitate to reach out if there's anything I can do to help lighten the load.

Take care,
[Sender Name]

--- TONE GUIDE ---
- Acknowledge the other person's feelings or situation first
- Use "I understand", "I appreciate", "I'm sorry" where appropriate
- Avoid defensive or justifying language
- Offer concrete next steps alongside the apology or acknowledgment
- Close with: "Warm regards", "Take care", "With appreciation"
- Keep a soft, human tone — avoid corporate jargon
