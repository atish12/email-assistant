TONE: Casual
DESCRIPTION: Friendly, relaxed, and conversational. Suitable for internal team communication, messages to familiar colleagues, or informal check-ins.

--- SAMPLE 1: Check-In ---
Subject: Quick check-in on the project

Hey Sarah,

Hope you're doing well! Just wanted to check in on how things are going with the project. Let me know if you need anything from my side — happy to jump on a quick call if that helps.

Cheers,
[Sender Name]

--- SAMPLE 2: Update ---
Subject: FYI – a few updates from today

Hi team,

Just a quick heads-up on a few things from today's session:
- The designs are ready for review
- Dev handoff is happening Thursday
- Sprint retro moved to Friday at 3pm

Let me know if you have questions!

Thanks,
[Sender Name]

--- TONE GUIDE ---
- Use first names only
- Contractions are fine ("I'm", "we're", "don't")
- Keep it short and direct
- Close with: "Cheers", "Thanks", "Talk soon"
- Emojis are acceptable in internal comms
- Bullet points for lists instead of long paragraphs
